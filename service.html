Make your shoes last longer

Simple habits that keep every pair looking new.

dry_cleaning

Clean the right way

Wipe sneakers with a soft brush and mild soap. Never put leather shoes in a washing machine.

air

Let them breathe

Rotate between two pairs and dry shoes in shade. Direct sun can crack soles and fade colours.

inventory_2

Store with care

Use shoe trees or stuffed paper to hold shape, and keep pairs in a cool, dry place.
