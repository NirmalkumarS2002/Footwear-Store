Frequently asked questions

How soon will you reply to my message?

We reply to emails and form messages within 24 hours. WhatsApp and phone queries are answered during store hours.

Can I try shoes in store before buying?

Yes. Visit our Chennai store for a free fitting and try as many pairs as you like.

How do I return or exchange a pair?

Message us with your order number within 30 days. We arrange a free pickup and send the new size.

Do you accept bulk orders for teams or schools?

Yes. Share your quantity and sizes through the form and we will send a special quote.
